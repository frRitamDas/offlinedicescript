Quick reality check

A fair, independent dice roll cannot be predicted from a player ID. This page does not read a game’s private data, alter outcomes, or guarantee a match. Use it for fun—not to make bets or decisions.

🧩 What the ID does

A local hash of your ID and guess number creates a repeatable sequence. The same ID starts the same guesses on this browser.

🎲 What it can’t do

IDs don’t reveal future randomness. Each color still has a 1 in 6 chance on a fair six-sided die.
